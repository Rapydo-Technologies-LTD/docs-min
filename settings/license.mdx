# License

View and manage your Rapydo license.

## Overview

The License tab displays your current license status and allows you to activate or renew your Rapydo license.

## License Status

The License Status section shows your current license details:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Current License | Your license type (e.g., Enterprise License) |
| Status | Whether your license is active and valid |
| Instance | The EC2 instance ID tied to this license |
| AWS Account | The AWS account associated with your license |
| Features | Your feature tier (e.g., Full Enterprise) |
| Max Instances | Maximum number of database instances you can monitor |
| Days Remaining | Time remaining until your license expires |
| Expiration Date | Exact date and time when your license expires |

## License Renewal

Click **Activate** to renew an expiring license or apply a new license key.

### Instance Information

The renewal form displays your instance details for easy reference when contacting sales:

<ParamField body="AWS Account ID" type="string">
  Your 12-digit AWS account number. Click **Copy** to copy it to your clipboard.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="EC2 Instance ID" type="string">
  The instance ID tied to your license. Click **Copy** to copy it to your clipboard.
</ParamField>

<Info>
  Provide these details to the sales team when requesting a license renewal.
</Info>

### Applying a License

<ParamField body="License Key or Trial Token" type="string" required>
  Paste your complete license key or trial token in the text area. License keys can be very long.
</ParamField>

Click **Renew Now** to apply the license or **Cancel** to close.
